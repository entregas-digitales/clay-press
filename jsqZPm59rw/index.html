How to use your templates

1

Open any PDF and go to File → Print. Set scale to 100% (Actual size). Never "Fit to page."

2

Each sheet has a 15 × 15 mm control square in the lower right corner. Measure it with a ruler after printing. If it is off, adjust the scale and reprint.

3

Cut along the black outline. For longer-lasting templates, laminate the sheet or mount it on cardstock before cutting.

4

Place the template on your clay slab and cut around it with a sharp knife held straight (90°).

Next step: print one template, cut it out and test it with a small slab. The control square tells you immediately if your printer is calibrated correctly. Once it checks out, you are ready to use all 100.
